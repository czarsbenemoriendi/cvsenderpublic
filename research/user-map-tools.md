# Tools for Building Good-Looking User Maps

Research date: 2026-09-02. All claims below were checked against primary sources (official product/pricing/integration pages, GitHub repos) on this date. "User map" is ambiguous, so this report covers all three standard interpretations:

1. **User journey map (UX)** — a timeline of a persona's actions, emotions, and pain points across touchpoints. Tools: whiteboard/template platforms (Miro, Mural, Figma Community) and dedicated CJM platforms (UXPressia, TheyDo, UXtweak).
2. **User story map (agile/backlog)** — Jeff Patton-style backbone → activities → stories → releases. Tools: Avion, StoriesOnBoard, FeatureMap, CardBoard. **Linear sync is called out explicitly** wherever verifiable, because the requester is a Linear workspace admin.
3. **User flow / diagram-as-code** — flowcharts of screens/decisions. Tools: Mermaid, Excalidraw, tldraw, draw.io, Whimsical, Eraser.

A short scan of AI-assisted newcomers (2025+) that generate journey/flow maps from prompts is included at the end.

> "Good-looking" is treated as a hard requirement. Visual-quality judgments are the author's assessments made from each vendor's official screenshots/gallery/product pages (linked inline) — they are opinions grounded in primary sources, not vendor claims, unless quoted.

## Interpretation 1 — User journey map (UX)

- **Miro** is the safest "good-looking + free start" choice: 63 dedicated customer-journey-map templates from named designers, a polished canvas, and a Free plan (3 editable boards) ([templates](https://miro.com/templates/customer-journey-maps/), [pricing](https://miro.com/pricing/)). Its Linear integration is real but gated: Linear's own directory confirms "Import, create and manage issues directly in Miro" with real-time sync, **available on Miro Business/Enterprise** ([linear.app/integrations/miro](https://linear.app/integrations/miro)); Miro's pricing page likewise lists "Jira, Asana, **Linear**, ClickUp, Azure … two-way sync" as a Business-tier feature ($20/member/mo billed yearly) ([miro.com/pricing](https://miro.com/pricing/)).
- **Mural** matches Miro's template depth (250+ expert templates, Free = 3 murals + unlimited members, Team+ $9.99/member/mo annual) ([pricing](https://www.mural.co/pricing)) but has **no Linear integration** anywhere in its integrations hub ([mural.co/integrations](https://www.mural.co/integrations)) — for a Linear-centric workflow that's disqualifying.
- **Figma Community templates** are free with a Starter account (unlimited drafts + templates, 150 AI credits/day) ([pricing](https://www.figma.com/pricing/)) and produce the highest-fidelity visuals of any option. Linear maintains a first-party Figma integration ("Create and link issues directly from Figma", listed under "Linear crafted") ([linear.app/integrations](https://linear.app/integrations)). Caveat: journey templates are static frames — no structured journey data, no automation. (Whether the requester uses Figma is unconfirmed.)
- **UXPressia** is the strongest *dedicated* CJM tool with a real free tier: 3 "designer-quality" journey maps + personas + impact maps, PNG export free; Pro $36/user/mo adds unlimited maps, PDF/CSV; Business $95/user/mo adds PPTX, branding, Jira/Azure DevOps integrations; AI journey-map generation is a marketed feature ([pricing](https://uxpressia.com/pricing)). No Linear.
- **TheyDo** is the 2025–2026 flagship of *AI-assisted journey management* (mining agent, analyst agent, journey summarization), but it's enterprise-only: 30-day trial, then sales-led Management/Strategic plans; PDF journey export only on Strategic ([pricing](https://www.theydo.com/pricing)). No Linear among its 53 integrations ([directory](https://www.theydo.com/integrations)).
- **UXtweak** is primarily a UX *research* platform (card sorting, usability tests; Free = 1 user, 15 responses/mo) ([pricing](https://www.uxtweak.com/pricing/)). Its journey-mapping presence is an excellent [guide + template collection](https://www.uxtweak.com/user-journey-map/); a dedicated visual journey-map builder is **not** listed among its current tools, so it's a research feeder, not a mapping tool.

## Interpretation 2 — User story map (agile), with Linear sync status

- **Avion is the only major dedicated story-mapping tool with a verified, live Linear integration.** Its dedicated page states: "Plan in Avion, deliver in Linear. Every story syncs to a Linear issue with estimates, projects, and labels intact, in real time," mapping stories→issues, releases→projects, tags→labels, story states→issue states, sizes→estimates ([avion.io](https://www.avion.io/user-story-mapping-with-linear/), [feature docs](https://help.avion.io/docs/integrations/backlog-tools/services/linear/features)). Cost: Starter $83/mo billed yearly ($99 monthly, unlimited users, 2 story maps, Slack only); **Linear integration starts at Business — $166/mo yearly ($199 monthly), 5 story maps**, PDF export; Enterprise adds Excel/CSV export and roadmaps ([pricing](https://www.avion.io/pricing/)). Free trial only, no permanent free tier.
- **StoriesOnBoard** is the per-seat budget option ($9–15/user/mo annual) with built-in AI (story/user-persona generation, 50k+ tokens/mo) and 14-day trial ([pricing](https://storiesonboard.com/pricing.html)). Its two-way sync targets **Jira, Azure DevOps, Trello, GitHub, Pivotal Tracker only — Linear is not offered** ([integrations](https://storiesonboard.com/integrations.html)).
- **FeatureMap** shut down in March 2024 and was relaunched by BigMentor as a deliberately no-frills, flat-price tool (€99/mo excl. VAT, unlimited users, France-hosted, Jira sync + open API) ([site](https://featuremap.co/)). No Linear; not a looker.
- **CardBoard**: `cardboard.tools` no longer resolves (DNS failure, checked 2026-09-02) — treat as discontinued.
- **DIY/OSS route**: [linearstories](https://github.com/stackingturtles/linearstories) (MIT, TypeScript CLI) keeps epics/user stories as markdown in your repo and two-way syncs them into Linear issues (epic/story hierarchy via parent/sub-issue), with a local "Project Atlas" visualization. It's a young, tiny project (0 stars at time of writing) — experimental, but the only free *and* Linear-native story-mapping path found.

## Interpretation 3 — User flow / diagram-as-code

- **Mermaid** ([mermaid.js.org](https://mermaid.js.org/), MIT) is the default text-to-flowchart choice — free, versionable, renders in GitHub/Markdown. Aesthetics are utilitarian out of the box; theming helps but rarely produces "presentation-grade" output.
- **Excalidraw** ([excalidraw.com](https://excalidraw.com/), MIT) delivers a genuinely attractive *hand-drawn* aesthetic — the official tagline is "Free, collaborative whiteboard • Hand-drawn look & feel." Best when you want charming over corporate; self-hostable.
- **tldraw** offers a crisp, modern canvas; the public app is free, but note the SDK's custom license: free for development/internal use, commercial license required for production embedding ([LICENSE.md](https://github.com/tldraw/tldraw/blob/main/LICENSE.md)).
- **draw.io / diagrams.net** ([diagrams.net](https://www.diagrams.net/), Apache-2.0 on [GitHub](https://github.com/jgraph/drawio/blob/dev/LICENSE)) is the free workhorse — unbeatable shape coverage (UML/BPMN/C4/AWS), desktop + web + VS Code, but the editor and default output read as utilitarian.
- **Whimsical** is the "good-looking" champion here: opinionated, minimal, consistently polished flowcharts (plus wireframes/sticky notes). Free = 50 board objects/editor/mo with watermarked exports; Pro $10/editor/mo (annual) removes limits/watermark; Business $20 adds SSO/SCIM ([pricing](https://whimsical.com/pricing)). No Linear integration (Slack only), though boards embed anywhere.
- **Eraser** is the strongest *diagram-as-code with taste*: a text DSL with modern default styling, 3,000+ tech icons, plus AI generation. Free tier includes diagram-as-code and 3 AI diagrams/mo; Starter $15/member/mo annual ($20 monthly); export PDF/PNG/SVG/MD on every plan ([pricing](https://www.eraser.io/pricing)).

## AI-assisted newcomers (2025+) worth knowing

- **Napkin AI** — paste text, get styled visuals (flows, timelines, journey-style infographics). The most generous free tier in this report: 500 AI credits *per week*, unlimited PNG/PDF export; Plus $9/person/mo, Pro $22/person/mo ([pricing](https://www.napkin.ai/pricing)).
- **Whimsical AI** — "Turn a simple text prompt into a complex flowchart … powered by Claude"; free sign-up includes 10 AI credits ([page](https://whimsical.com/ai/ai-text-to-flowchart)).
- **Eraser AI / DiagramGPT** — prompt→diagram generation inside the diagram-as-code tool ([docs](https://docs.eraser.io/reference/generate-diagram-from-prompt), [product](https://www.eraser.io/ai)).
- **TheyDo AI agents** — mining/analyst agents and journey summarization aimed at enterprise journey management ([pricing](https://www.theydo.com/pricing)).
- **StoriesOnBoard AI** — generates story maps, personas, user stories, acceptance criteria from prompts, with monthly token quotas on every plan ([pricing](https://storiesonboard.com/pricing.html)).
- **UXPressia AI** — AI customer-journey-map and persona generation marketed as a core feature ([pricing page nav](https://uxpressia.com/pricing)).
- **Miro AI** — diagram/table/summary generation, 25 AI credits/member/mo on Starter, 50 on Business ([pricing](https://miro.com/pricing/)).

## Verdict

- **Journey map (UX): Miro.** It's the only tool that combines genuinely attractive, template-backed journey maps (63 CJM templates incl. designer-made ones), a real free tier to start on, and a verified Linear path (official Linear⇄Miro integration on Miro Business, $20/member/mo yearly). Runner-up: **UXPressia** if you want a dedicated, "designer-quality" CJM tool with a permanent free tier (3 maps) and don't need Linear; **Figma Community** templates win on fidelity if you already live in Figma.
- **Story map tied to Linear: Avion** — it is the *only* dedicated story-mapping tool whose Linear sync could be verified from primary sources (real-time, two-way, estimates/projects/labels mapped), and its story-map UX is clean and purpose-built. The catch for a solo dev is pricing: Linear sync sits in the $166/mo-yearly Business tier with no free plan, so validate on the trial first. Pragmatic alternatives: **Miro Business** (story-map templates + Linear cards at $20/mo) or the free/OSS **linearstories** CLI if you're happy with markdown-as-source-of-truth.
- **User flow / diagram: Whimsical** for the best-looking flowcharts with a workable free tier and AI prompt-to-flowchart; **Excalidraw** if you want free/OSS with a charming hand-drawn look; **Eraser** if you want diagram-as-code without Mermaid's utilitarian aesthetics. Use **Mermaid** or **draw.io** only when "free and boring" is the requirement, not the look.
- **Wildcard: Napkin AI** — for prompt-generated, genuinely polished visuals (free 500 credits/week), it's the fastest route from text to a presentable map-like graphic, though output is presentation art, not structured/linked data.
